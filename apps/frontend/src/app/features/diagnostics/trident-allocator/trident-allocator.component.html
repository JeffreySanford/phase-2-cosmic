FSP Allocator Simulator

FSP (Frequency Slice Processor) units are the compute resources in the telescope array that process chunks of the incoming radio frequency band — each FSP handles a slice of the spectrum. Before a real observation runs, this tool answers: "If I schedule this block, will the compute resources be available and non-conflicting?"

Submit a SchedulingBlock to validate FSP capacity, detect subarray contention, and receive an FspAllocationPlan or typed conflict diagnostics. Requires the local simulator at http://localhost:7777.
